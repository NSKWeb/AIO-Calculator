About This Calculator

This asphalt calculator helps you estimate the amount of asphalt needed for paving projects. It calculates based on standard asphalt density of approximately 145 pounds per cubic foot. Always add 5-10% extra to your order to account for waste and compaction.

Disclaimer: This calculator provides estimates only. Actual requirements may vary based on specific project conditions, asphalt mix design, and compaction methods. Consult with a professional for accurate project planning.
