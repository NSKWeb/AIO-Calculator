About This Calculator

This speed calculator estimates average speed based on distance traveled and time taken. It's useful for calculating average speed during road trips, testing vehicle performance, or estimating travel times.

Note: This calculates average speed, not instantaneous speed. For accurate measurements of maximum speed, use a GPS device or speedometer.
