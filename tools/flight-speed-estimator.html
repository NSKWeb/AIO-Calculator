About This Calculator

This flight speed estimator calculates the average speed of an aircraft based on the total distance traveled and flight duration. This includes time spent taxiing, taking off, cruising, and landing.

Typical Aircraft Speeds:

  • Commercial jets: 500-600 mph (cruising speed)
  • Regional jets: 400-500 mph
  • Propeller aircraft: 200-350 mph
  • Helicopters: 100-160 mph

Disclaimer: This calculator provides average speed including all phases of flight. Actual cruising speed may be higher. For aviation purposes, always use official flight data.
