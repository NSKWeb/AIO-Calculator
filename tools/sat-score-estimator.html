About This Calculator

This SAT score estimator converts raw scores (number of correct answers) to estimated scaled scores based on the current SAT format. The SAT consists of two sections: Math and Reading & Writing, each scored on a scale of 200-800, for a total possible score of 1600.

Score Ranges:

  • Math: 44 questions, raw score 0-58
  • Reading & Writing: 54 questions, raw score 0-96
  • Each section: 200-800 points
  • Total score: 400-1600 points

Good to Know: Actual scaled scores may vary slightly depending on the specific test form difficulty (equating). This calculator provides estimates based on average conversion tables.

Disclaimer: This tool provides estimates only. Actual SAT scores are determined by the College Board using their specific equating process. Use this for practice test scoring only.
