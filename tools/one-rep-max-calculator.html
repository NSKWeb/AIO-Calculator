About This Calculator

This one rep max (1RM) calculator uses the Epley formula to estimate your maximum weight for a single repetition based on a submaximal lift. This is useful for setting training goals and determining appropriate weights for different training intensities.

Formula: 1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps/30)

Training Zones:

  • 95-100%: Max effort / Testing
  • 90-95%: Strength / Power
  • 80-90%: Hypertrophy & Strength
  • 70-80%: Hypertrophy
  • 60-70%: Endurance
  • 50-60%: Warm-up / Technique

Important: Actual 1RM may vary. Always use proper form and consider working with a qualified trainer for heavy lifts. Never attempt a true 1RM without proper supervision and safety equipment.

Disclaimer: This calculator provides estimates only. Actual lifting capacity may vary. Always consult with a fitness professional before attempting heavy lifts.
