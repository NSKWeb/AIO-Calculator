Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

Introduction

At CalcTools, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website and its free online calculators and generators.

No Personal Data Collection

We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information including:

  • Names and email addresses
  • IP addresses
  • Location data
  • Device information
  • Usage statistics
  • Any form of user identification

How Our Tools Work

All calculations and data processing happen entirely in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. This means:

  • Your input data never leaves your device
  • No data is sent to any server
  • All calculations are performed locally
  • Results are generated instantly in your browser

Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics services. Our website may use basic local storage for:

  • Remembering your theme preferences (if applicable)
  • Temporarily storing calculation results (optional feature)

Any data stored in local storage remains on your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party services that collect your personal data, including:

  • Analytics platforms (Google Analytics, etc.)
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • External data processing services

User-Generated Content

Our generators (such as the Couple Name Generator, Headcanon Generator, and Surname Generator) create content based on your input. However:

  • Your input is not stored or transmitted
  • Generated content is displayed only to you
  • No content is saved to any database
  • There is no user account system to track your activity

Website Security

We take reasonable measures to ensure our website is secure:

  • HTTPS encryption for all connections
  • Regular security updates
  • Secure coding practices
  • No user data to protect (privacy by design)

Children's Privacy

Our website is suitable for users of all ages. Since we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated revision date.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information for you to access, correct, or delete. Your use of our website remains completely anonymous.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through our website.

Conclusion

CalcTools is designed with privacy as a fundamental principle. We believe that useful tools should be available without compromising your personal data. Your trust is important to us, and we will continue to maintain these high privacy standards.
